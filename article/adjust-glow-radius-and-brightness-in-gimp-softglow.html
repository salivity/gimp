Adjust Glow Radius and Brightness in GIMP Softglow

The Softglow filter in GIMP provides a simple way to create atmospheric, luminous lighting effects by intensifying the brightest highlights of an image. By adjusting the glow radius and brightness threshold controls, you can precisely control how far the illumination spreads and which pixel values produce the glow. This guide explains how to access the Softglow tool and properly configure these two core parameters to achieve subtle or dramatic lighting effects.

Accessing the Softglow Filter

  1. Open your image in GIMP and ensure the target layer is selected in the Layers panel.
  2. Navigate to the top menu and select Filters > Light and Shadow > Softglow.
  3. In the dialog window that appears, ensure the Preview box is checked so you can observe the changes in real time.

Adjusting the Glow Radius

The Glow radius slider defines the extent and spread of the light blooming out from bright regions.

  • How it works: It behaves similarly to a Gaussian blur applied specifically to the highlights. A small radius confines the illumination tightly around the edges of bright spots, while a larger radius casts a wide, diffused mist across neighboring pixels.
  • How to set it: Drag the Glow radius slider or type a value into the adjacent input field. For crisp, subtle highlights (such as on jewelry, metallic reflections, or eyes), keep the value low (around 1 to 10). For a dreamy, ethereal, or vintage portrait effect, increase the radius (values of 20 to 50 or higher).

Adjusting the Brightness Threshold

The Brightness slider functions as a luminance threshold, dictating which parts of your image are intense enough to trigger the glow.

  • How it works: Increasing the brightness setting restricts the effect so that only pure whites and strong highlights glow. Lowering the brightness broadens the threshold, allowing midtones and darker shades to emit light as well.
  • How to set it: Move the Brightness slider to regulate light sensitivity. If your image looks washed out or overexposed, increase the brightness slider to isolate the effect to the brightest light sources. If you want a hazy, overall dream-like wash across the entire scene, reduce the brightness so midtones are pulled into the glow.

Finalizing the Effect

Balance both controls by starting with a moderate Glow radius to set the spread, then fine-tuning the Brightness slider until only the desired areas emit light. You can also adjust the Sharpness slider in the same dialog to make the light transition softer or more defined. Once satisfied with the preview, click OK to apply the Softglow filter to your layer.
