How to Flip All Layers Horizontally in GIMP

Flipping a complex, multi-layered project horizontally in GIMP does not require merging your layers or flipping each element individually. By using the main Image Transform menu instead of individual layer tools, you can instantly mirror your entire canvas along the vertical axis while keeping every layer, mask, and text element fully intact and editable.

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Open Your Project: Load your multi-layered composition in GIMP. Ensure you are ready to mirror the global canvas.
  2. Access the Image Menu: Click on the Image tab located in the top menu bar (between Edit and Layer).
  3. Navigate to Transform: Hover your cursor over the Transform sub-menu to open the transformation options.
  4. Select Flip Horizontally: Click on Flip Horizontally.

What Happens to Your Composition

Applying the horizontal flip through the Image menu acts globally rather than locally:

  • All Layers Mirror Simultaneously: Every layer—visible, hidden, locked, or linked—will be flipped horizontally across the center of the canvas.
  • Layer Hierarchy and Blend Modes Remain Intact: Your layer order, opacity settings, layer groups, and blending modes do not change.
  • Layer Masks and Paths Follow: Any attached layer masks, selections, or vector paths adjust automatically to match the mirrored orientation.
  • Canvas Dimensions Are Preserved: The original boundaries and aspect ratio of your canvas remain unchanged.

Using Image > Transform > Flip Horizontally ensures a seamless global mirror, avoiding the tedious process of using the standard Flip Tool (Shift + F) on individual layers one by one.
