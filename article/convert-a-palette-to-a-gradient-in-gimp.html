Convert a Palette to a Gradient in GIMP

Converting a custom color palette into a continuous, smooth gradient in GIMP allows you to blend your curated color schemes for backgrounds, shading, and digital illustrations. This guide walks you through the exact menu commands required to transform any saved palette into a fully functional, smooth gradient using GIMP's dockable dialogs.

Step 1: Open the Palettes Dialog

If your Palettes tab is not currently visible in your workspace, open it via the main application menu:

  1. Click Windows in the top menu bar.
  2. Hover over Dockable Dialogs.
  3. Select Palettes.

Step 2: Locate Your Saved Palette

Scroll through the Palettes dialog list to find the saved palette you want to convert. Click on it once to ensure it is selected.

Step 3: Execute the Conversion

  1. Right-click the selected palette in the list to open its context menu.
  2. Click Palette to Gradient.

GIMP automatically generates a new gradient that interpolates smoothly across each color entry in the palette, matching the order from left to right.

Step 4: Access and Use the New Gradient

To verify and use your newly created gradient:

  1. Go to Windows > Dockable Dialogs > Gradients.
  2. Locate the gradient, which will automatically share the same name as the original palette.
  3. Select the Gradient Tool from the Tools menu (Tools > Paint Tools > Gradient or press G).
  4. Choose your new gradient from the tool options and drag across your canvas to apply the smooth transition.

Step 5: Adjusting Smoothness (Optional)

If your generated gradient shows sharp boundaries instead of a continuous blend, you can refine it:

  1. In the Gradients dialog, right-click your new gradient and select Edit Gradient.
  2. In the Gradient Editor, right-click the segments, choose Blending Function for Selection, and set it to Linear or Curved to ensure seamless, continuous color transitions.
