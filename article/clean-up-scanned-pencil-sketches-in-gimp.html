Clean Up Scanned Pencil Sketches in GIMP

Cleaning up scanned pencil sketches in GIMP prepares traditional artwork for seamless digital inking and coloring. This guide outlines the essential workflow to eliminate paper textures, remove graphite smudges, enhance line contrast, and isolate the drawing onto a transparent layer. Following these steps ensures your original line work serves as a crisp, non-destructive guide for digital illustration.

1. Import and Crop the Scan

  • Open GIMP and go to File > Open to load your scanned image (ideally scanned at 300 to 600 DPI).
  • Select the Crop Tool (Shift + C) from the toolbox.
  • Drag a box around the sketch area to eliminate unused margins and press Enter to crop.

2. Remove Color Casts (Desaturate)

Scanners often introduce faint blue, yellow, or gray color casts to white paper.

  • Go to Colors > Desaturate > Desaturate...
  • Choose the Luminance mode and click OK. This converts the sketch into a clean grayscale image.

3. Boost Contrast with Levels

Enhancing the contrast makes the pencil lines darker while pushing off-white paper tones to pure white.

  • Navigate to Colors > Levels...
  • In the histogram, locate the Input Levels sliders:
    • Drag the black slider (left) toward the center to darken the pencil lines.
    • Drag the white slider (right) toward the center to force the paper background to become pure white.
    • Adjust the gray slider (middle) to preserve fine line details without exposing unwanted paper grain.
  • Click OK once the lines are solid and the background appears completely white.

4. Remove Smudges and Artifacts

  • Select the Eraser Tool (Shift + E) or the Paintbrush Tool (P) set to pure white (#FFFFFF).
  • Set brush hardness to a moderate level.
  • Paint over any stray marks, scanning artifacts, or remaining graphite smudges outside the primary drawing.

5. Convert Lines to a Transparent Layer

Isolating the pencil strokes allows you to ink digitally on separate layers above or below the original sketch.

  • Go to Layer > Transparency > Color to Alpha...
  • Ensure the Color is set to white (#FFFFFF).
  • Click OK. The white background will disappear, leaving only the dark pencil marks on a transparent grid.

6. Prepare the Layer for Digital Inking

  • Rename the sketch layer to "Pencil Sketch" in the Layers panel.
  • Lower the layer's Opacity slider to around 30% - 50%.
  • Click the Lock pixels icon in the Layers panel to avoid accidentally drawing on the original sketch.
  • Click the New Layer button to create a transparent layer above your sketch, name it "Inking", and begin tracing your clean line art using your preferred brush tools.
