How to Reset Canvas Rotation in GIMP

Rotating the canvas is a convenient feature for digital artists in GIMP, but returning the view to its default angle is essential when reviewing your work. This article explains how to instantly reset canvas rotation back to zero degrees using keyboard shortcuts, modifier keys, and menu navigation.

The primary modifier key used to reset canvas rotation in GIMP is Shift, specifically through the ! (exclamation mark) shortcut, which on standard keyboard layouts is entered by pressing Shift + 1. Pressing this shortcut immediately resets any active canvas rotation and flipping, snapping the view straight back to zero degrees.

Methods to Reset Canvas Rotation

1. The Instant Keyboard Shortcut

Press the ! key (Shift + 1 on most keyboards) while working on your canvas. GIMP will immediately clear all rotation angles and restore the default orientation without opening any dialogs or menus.

2. The View Menu

If you prefer using application menus instead of key combinations:

  1. Click View in the top menu bar.
  2. Hover over Flip & Rotate (180°).
  3. Select Reset Flip and Rotate.

3. The Status Bar

At the bottom of the GIMP workspace, you can find the rotation field displaying the current angle in degrees. Click this field, type 0, and press Enter to bring the canvas back to its standard upright alignment.
