How to Use the Photocopy Filter in GIMP

This article provides an overview of the Photocopy filter in GIMP, detailing how it works, its adjustable parameters, and its primary use cases in image editing. Readers will learn how this artistic tool converts photographs into high-contrast, toner-style black-and-white graphics that mimic physical photocopier prints.

What Is the Photocopy Filter?

The Photocopy filter in GIMP (located under Filters > Artistic > Photocopy) is a specialized artistic effect designed to simulate the look of a printed photocopy. It analyzes the tones and edges of an image, reduces the color palette, and creates strong dark outlines combined with stippled mid-tones and clean highlights, replicating the visual imperfections and high contrast of toner ink on paper.

How the Photocopy Filter Works

The filter functions by evaluating the local contrast and edge boundaries within the active layer or selection. Instead of a simple grayscale conversion, it performs edge detection and tonal thresholding to separate the image into distinct shadow, mid-tone, and highlight areas based on the active foreground and background colors (defaulting to black and white).

Key Parameters and Settings

When applying the Photocopy filter, four primary sliders allow you to customize the output:

  • Mask Radius: Controls the size of the pixel neighborhood analyzed for contrast. Lower values generate fine, localized details, while higher values create broader, softer blocks of shadow and light.
  • Sharpness: Determines the clarity and definition of the detected edges. Increasing sharpness produces distinct, crisp ink lines, while reducing it softens transitions.
  • Percent Black: Adjusts the amount of dark toner added to the image. Higher values expand the dark areas, darkening shadows and thickening outlines.
  • Percent White: Regulates the intensity and distribution of the highlight areas. Higher values push more mid-tones into pure white, increasing overall brightness.

Common Use Cases

  • Comic and Line Art Creation: Quickly converts standard photography into line-heavy bases for digital painting or comic book panels.
  • Grunge and Vintage Graphic Design: Generates retro print aesthetics ideal for zines, punk flyers, and album artwork.
  • Stencil and Screen Printing Prep: Simplifies complex continuous-tone images into distinct levels suitable for screen exposure and stencil cutting.
