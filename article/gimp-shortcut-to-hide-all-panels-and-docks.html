GIMP Shortcut to Hide All Panels and Docks

Working on intricate image editing tasks in GIMP often requires maximum canvas visibility without the clutter of toolboxes, layers, and dialogs. This guide explains the essential keyboard shortcut used to instantly hide and show all dockable windows and panels simultaneously in GIMP, outlines how to toggle this view, and covers troubleshooting tips if the shortcut does not respond.

The default keyboard shortcut to toggle all dockable panels on and off in GIMP is the Tab key.

Pressing Tab while your canvas is active immediately hides the Toolbox, Tool Options, Layers panel, and any other dockable dialogs you have open. Pressing Tab a second time restores all panels to their exact prior positions. This allows you to inspect your artwork cleanly without altering your customized workspace layout.

How to Use the Shortcut

  1. Click anywhere on your active image canvas to ensure GIMP is focused on the workspace rather than a text field or numeric input box.
  2. Press the Tab key on your keyboard to hide all open docks.
  3. Press the Tab key again whenever you need your tools and panels returned.

Menu Alternative

If you prefer using the mouse rather than keyboard shortcuts, you can access the same command through the standard menu:

  • Navigate to the top menu bar and select Windows.
  • Click Hide Docks to toggle the visibility of your panels.

Creating an Absolute Distraction-Free Workspace

To maximize your view beyond just hiding panels, combine the dock toggle with GIMP's fullscreen mode:

  • Press F11 to enter fullscreen mode, removing the title bar, menu bar, and operating system taskbar.
  • Press Tab to hide all docks, leaving only your artwork visible across the entire display.
  • Press F11 and Tab again at any time to return to the standard interface.

Troubleshooting the Tab Shortcut

If pressing the Tab key does not hide your panels, check the following solutions:

  • Clear Active Inputs: If your cursor is active inside a text layer, brush size box, or numerical slider, pressing Tab will navigate between input fields instead of hiding panels. Click the canvas or press the Esc key to unfocus the field, then press Tab.
  • Verify Key Bindings: If the shortcut was accidentally unmapped, open Edit > Keyboard Shortcuts, search for "Hide Docks", ensure the action is assigned to the Tab key, and save your changes.
